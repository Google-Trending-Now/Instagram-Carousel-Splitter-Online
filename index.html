Best Instagram Carousel Dimensions & Export Tips

Image dimensions matter when creating an Instagram carousel. For portrait feed images, 1080 × 1350 pixels is a popular choice because its 4:5 format takes up more vertical screen space than a square image.

For a multi-slide panorama, every slide should use matching dimensions and align correctly with its neighbors. Our splitter keeps the slices in their original order.

Format Dimensions Best Use
4:5 Portrait 1080 × 1350 Instagram feed
1:1 Square 1080 × 1080 Square carousel
3:4 Portrait 1080 × 1440 Tall portrait layouts

Keep important details away from cut lines

Before exporting, check the preview carefully. Avoid placing faces, text, logos, or other important subjects directly on the vertical boundaries between slides.

Choose the right export format

JPG is a practical choice for photographs and smaller file sizes. PNG is useful when you want lossless output, especially for graphics, illustrations, and images with text.

Both formats are generated directly in your browser. Your original image remains unchanged.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about splitting photos for Instagram? Here are some helpful answers.

How do I split a panoramic photo for Instagram?

Upload your panoramic image to the Instagram carousel splitter online. Choose your slide count and preferred dimensions, preview the cuts, and download your images.

Does splitting an image reduce its quality?

The tool processes images using the browser's Canvas API. JPG images are exported at 95% quality, while PNG provides lossless output. Resizing to a smaller resolution can reduce detail compared with your original image.

How many slides can I create at once?

You can split one photograph into 2 to 10 slides. Every slide is generated in the correct order so you can upload them as an Instagram carousel.

Are my uploaded photos private and secure?

Yes. All image processing happens locally in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to our server, and the tool does not require an account.

Can I use this tool on an iPhone or Android phone?

Yes. The splitter works in modern mobile browsers. Open this page in Safari or Chrome, select a photo from your camera roll, and create your slides.

Can I download all Instagram carousel slides together?

Yes. Use the Download All Slides button to create a ZIP file containing every slide. If the ZIP library is unavailable, the tool will offer individual downloads one after another.

What image size should I use for Instagram?

The 1080 × 1350 pixel portrait format is a popular Instagram feed option. Square images can use 1080 × 1080 pixels. Make sure all slides in a carousel use matching dimensions.
